Many Zoom files, one LMS file

Add your Zoom CSV, or several. Whatever columns it has are matched into the LMS format, and the file is saved for you. Nothing is uploaded.

    name from Name, country_code from CountryCode
    number_without_country_code and phone_number from Phone
    email from emailid, video from session
    lead_value from zoomads, oto_non_oto from zoomid
    platform from msg2, profession from msg3, amount from amount
    order_id from wsdt, enrolled at from time
    Columns your file does not have are left blank, with no error. If zoomads or zoomid is missing, WN_h81UmFwmQnCoQ-55QxELuQ and 997 7254 2342 are used.
